Use case: stylized-concept / logo-brand.
Create one polished original mascot concept presentation image for a fresh grocery supermarket serving the Donghua University community in Shanghai. The website audience is university students and staff, an excellent mature university software course project, not a children's brand. The character's tentative name is “小锦” (Xiao Jin), a friendly river otter. Do NOT imitate Hema's hippopotamus or any existing mascot.

Design: a distinctive elegant warm grey-brown river otter with small rounded ears, a pale oat-cream muzzle and chest, a long tapered otter tail visibly curling to one side, small calm dark eyes and a subtle confident friendly smile. An adult-oriented brand character with clear memorable silhouette and restrained proportions: head about one third of total height, no giant sparkling eyes, no blush cheeks. Compact upright pose, simplified paws. It wears a small deep brocade-red canvas grocery apron (approximately #A0021F), with a tasteful woven seam/check texture only on a tiny patch, alluding gently to Donghua's textile heritage. No university emblem, no invented university seal. The fresh-food connection is a plain oat-coloured reusable market tote containing a spring onion, leafy green vegetable and one tomato, drawn simply and attractively. Red is a restrained accent, the mascot remains mostly natural grey-brown and cream.

Medium: premium clean character illustration with subtle tactile matte gouache/paper shading, crisp simplified shapes and controlled fine contour; commercial mascot identity quality, easy to reproduce as a small website spot illustration. Not photorealistic, not a glossy 3D vinyl toy, not anime, not clip art, no heavy shadows. It should feel designed, warm, capable and a little clever, not babyish.

Composition: a spacious landscape concept sheet on plain warm white. One large full-body main mascot at left, standing with the grocery tote and a relaxed welcoming paw. At right, three smaller separated consistent versions of THE SAME mascot: 1) greeting with one paw, 2) gently checking a grocery order on a small clipboard, 3) peeking above a simple empty grocery basket with an encouraging expression. Keep facial identity, proportions and apron identical between poses. Each illustration isolated with generous breathing room. A small sophisticated heading “小锦” and tiny secondary text “校园生鲜 · 吉祥物概念” only. The main character should dominate, no web page layouts or UI chrome, no logos, no explanatory paragraphs, no badges, no watermarks. High quality coherent art direction, warm and refined.
